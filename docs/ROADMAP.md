# CART Roadmap 


## Overview 

This roadmap outlines the planned development of the CART tool up to mid-September 2026.  

While continuing to support the auditing of cross-government guidance for business on GOV.UK, the focus will also include:  

- support for DBT-specific content, across all content types 

- exploring how to align the auditing tool with new and emerging content lifecycle tools and processes at DBT 


## Objectives 

- Iterate how CART supports auditing of cross-government guidance for business  

- Produce recommendations for how CART could be expanded beyond the current data environment in DBT and Streamlit 

- Expand CART to support auditing of all DBT content 

- Produce recommendations for how CART should align with other tools and processes being developed as part of DBT's content operations work 


## Milestones 


### 1. Content pipelines 

- Enhance the existing pipeline to continue supporting cross-government auditing of business guidance 

- Develop a new DBT-specific pipeline to support all DBT content 


### 2. Red flag rules 

- Implement new cross-government flag rules (currently in development by DBT performance analysts) 

- Adapt and extend rules for the wider range of document types in the DBT-specific content needs 


### 3. Decision tracking 

- Define an approach for tracking content review decisions 

- Establish mechanisms to feed decision data back into CART to support continuous improvement 


### 4. Dashboard and workflow improvements 

- Improve overall usability and feature set of the CART dashboard 

- Improve usability of CART outputs (e.g. CSV exports, work trackers) 

- Refine content review workflows that use CART outputs and user journeys 


### 5. Role in DBT content ecosystem 

- Explore CART's potential role within DBT's wider content operations work   

- Explore integration opportunities with existing tools and processes 

- Produce a recommendations report for future integration


### 6. Creating shareable reports

- Exploring developing a reproducible report generation

- The reports would be summary recommendations from the output of CART

- Shareable to stakeholders and content owners

  

### 7. Platform exploration (post-Streamlit) 

- Gather long-term requirements for CART 

- Assess current limitations of the Streamlit-based implementation 

- Explore alternative platforms and architectures 

- Provide a clear recommendation for future replatforming 

- Consider alignment with other cross-government auditing initiatives, e.g. GDS  


### 8. LLM-assisted analysis

- Support ongoing work to explore how LLMs can assess the factual accuracy of content at scale  

- Collaborate with related initiatives where appropriate 

- Delivery not guaranteed within the next three months 


## Notes 

- Items are prioritised for the next phase of work but may evolve based on user needs and resourcing
